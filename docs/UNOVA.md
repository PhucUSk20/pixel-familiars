# Unova Dragons

Current positions use the [three-tier group layout introduced in 0.30.0](ARENA-LAYOUT.md). Earlier Demo GIFs below illustrate the skill choreography.

Version 0.27.0 adds **Unova Dragons** to Legendary Arena. Reshiram and Zekrom arrive together, charge an overhead fire orb and an electric discharge, then clash Blue Flare with an electric signature attack. Kyurem appears through an ice fracture, launches four curved homing energy missiles, then charges and fires a large mouth-origin energy sphere.

Each encounter independently chooses **White Kyurem (50%)** or **Black Kyurem (50%)**. Kyurem absorbs Reshiram for White or Zekrom for Black; the absorbed pet disappears into the fusion while the other dragon remains as its opponent. The fusion combines icy particles with fire or electricity, collides with the opponent's signature attack, then separates. All three original bodies return and depart after 47.5 seconds.

All five bodies use the supplied 128px GIF source standard, with attached wing, head, arm, leg and tail joints. Zekrom's turbine gathers blue electricity during its signature charge. Reshiram's plume has a blue/turquoise flame body and bright core. Kyurem's missiles launch from its back, spread outward and curve onto both opponents with matching trails and local impacts.

Resident duels continue during the encounter. Space/Time and Sun/Moon can remain present. The new encounter uses the existing 280px sky cap; Kyurem moves to a distinct upper lane when other visitors are present, keeping it clear of Giratina. Arceus or Ultra interrupts fusion, restores the absorbed host and recruits all three dragons into the combined assault. Judgment and the Ultra sky counter cause visible hit motion and recoil; visitors then depart. With every visitor and both bosses present, thirteen pets can participate in the Arceus sequence. **Rest** cancels every encounter, including absorption.

Auto visits have a randomized 120–240 second cooldown and defer during other boss activity. No model requests or tokens are used. Recorded previews are labeled **Demo**.

## References and interpretation

- [Official Pokémon retrospective](https://www.pokemon.com/de/news/feiere-25-jahre-pokemon-mit-unvergesslichen-momenten-aus-der-einall-region) describes the DNA Splicers and Kyurem's absorption fusion with either Reshiram or Zekrom. This scene restores the host after each visit; it does not fuse both dragons at once.
- [User ordinary Reshiram reference](https://ru.pinterest.com/pin/371687775517725486/) shows a fire orb charging above its head while its tail burns below the body.
- [Official VGC preview](https://www.pokemon.com/us/features/vgc-2016-championship-series-preview-part-2) identifies Reshiram's Blue Flare and Fusion Flare. [User Blue Flare reference](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/11/b2/f7/11b2f79f2b3c806e1b6689a65fe58dd7.gif) shows the glowing mouth charge and turquoise flame plume.
- [User Zekrom signature reference](https://makeagif.com/gif/zekrom-skill-pocket-incoming-ExH02s) informs the bright blue electric beam, lightning and collision flash.
- [User Kyurem pursuit reference](https://makeagif.com/gif/pokemon-generations-kyurem-vs-reshiram-vF-Xky) shows magenta homing projectiles. Its excerpt includes a powered Kyurem form; the back-launch choreography is adapted to the initial Kyurem appearance as requested.
- [User Kyurem ultimate reference](https://64.media.tumblr.com/tumblr_mc8kceMX7C1rv6iido1_500.gifv) shows an orange mouth-origin energy sphere. The scene preserves that animation and adds ice highlights; it does not claim that the visible orange orb is Glaciate.

The encounter is a local cinematic adaptation of these visual references, rather than a recreation of a complete canonical battle.
