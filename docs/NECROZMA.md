# Necrozma: restore the Blinding One

Current positions use the [three-tier group layout introduced in 0.30.0](ARENA-LAYOUT.md). Earlier Demo GIFs below illustrate the skill choreography.

Press **Necrozma** in Legendary Arena. Lunala and Solgaleo arrive if absent; resident battles continue. Necrozma can also interrupt a natural Sun / Moon encounter after its longer random cooldown. Rest cancels the scene. Summoning Arceus before Ultra releases an absorbed host immediately. Once Ultra is present it stays and joins the shared assault against Arceus.

The absorption and restoration scene adapts the Sun & Moon anime sequence:

1. Necrozma enters through an Ultra Wormhole and captures Lunala, becoming Dawn Wings.
2. Pink photon volleys and a cyan/white charged beam confront Solgaleo. Solgaleo strikes and frees Lunala.
3. Necrozma captures Solgaleo, becoming Dusk Mane. Its prism/mane charge releases radial light and a powerful beam against Lunala.
4. Lunala helps release Solgaleo. Both freed partners gather lunar/solar light and send it into the depleted base Necrozma.
5. With overwhelming light restored, Necrozma returns to Ultra. A golden chest charge feeds a sky sphere, then becomes the boss of a combined battle. All present pets attack its prism barrier, then it answers with Light That Burns the Sky and all visitors depart.

**Lore boundary:** Ultra is a form restoration/transformation, not an evolution that requires consuming both hosts. In the games either fusion can use Ultra Burst with Ultranecrozium Z. In the anime, the Ultra Guardians and Alola provide Z-Power to release Nebby; then Solgaleo and Lunala's Z-Moves restore Ultra Necrozma. The Arena compresses this into the two pets supplying light; its visitor duel, timings and final sky burst are a visual adaptation, not a literal episode reenactment.

## Ultra battle (0.26.0)

**Summon Ultra** skips absorption and starts the restored form directly. Space / Time, Giratina and Sun / Moon can share the battlefield. When Ultra is the sole boss, all present pets unite against its golden prism barrier, then it counters with a huge chest-fed sky sphere, a wide white/gold light pillar and a detonation wave across the Arena. Impact flashes and recoil accompany the counter; the creation trio uses its shields.

Arceus can join a boss encounter (35% chance), and Ultra can follow an Arceus arrival (30% chance). A lone Ultra also has a 65% chance to call Space / Time. Manual buttons can produce either order. **Arceus takes priority**: Ultra becomes an ally and fires a stronger golden stream simultaneously with every other pet at Arceus's barrier. All ten attackers are targeted by Judgment if all five visitors are present. Ultra, Arceus and the temporary visitors leave together after the counter. Rest cancels all pending arrivals.

These boss and alliance battles are Arena choreography, not a claim that the anime depicts this matchup. The final move follows the [supplied game reference](https://i.redd.it/d79zzqdoit1c1.gif)'s converging chest light, colossal orb and sky pillar rather than an empty-center demonstration beam.
