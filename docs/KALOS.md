# Kalos Guardians

Current positions use the [three-tier group layout introduced in 0.30.0](ARENA-LAYOUT.md). Earlier Demo GIFs below illustrate the skill choreography.

**Kalos Guardians** summons Xerneas and Yveltal into a 44.6-second cinematic. Auto also schedules occasional visits; Rest cancels the encounter. These scenes are labeled Demo and do not represent measured Codex activity.

The pair first exchange light/plasma orbs, then clash Xerneas's seven-color beam and Yveltal's red-violet energy. Xerneas gathers orbiting lights around its antlers; Yveltal emerges from a red shadow cocoon and unfolds its wings before charging an electric orb.

Zygarde Complete intervenes after the duel, dashes at both combatants with Extreme Speed, then uses green Core Enforcer energy with a visible Z trace. It transforms using the supplied Mega sprite, gathers a white-violet core through three hexagonal gates and releases a large purple cannon. Both opponents recoil; all three depart.

The four supplied 128px source bodies retain their native colors and use attached articulation at the Arena's existing sprite scale. Mega/Omega is the user's cinematic form label; this encounter and its victory sequence are a local adaptation, not a recreation of a canonical battle.

Resident battles continue during the encounter. Kalos can coexist with the other visitor groups without exceeding their shared sky expansion cap. Arceus or Ultra interrupts the duel and recruits all three actors. Zygarde keeps its current form: interruption before evolution does not force Mega. The largest combined scene has fifteen Ultra opponents or sixteen attackers against Arceus.
